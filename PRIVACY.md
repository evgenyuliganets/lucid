# Lucid privacy policy

Effective: 17 November 2026

Lucid is a smart alarm for an Android phone and a Galaxy Watch, published on
Google Play by Greety Apps. This policy explains what the app reads and where
that data goes.

## Summary

Lucid has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it
reads stays on your phone and your watch.

## What Lucid reads and why

- **Movement and heart rate (watch).** During your wake window, and up to
  30 minutes before it, the watch app reads the accelerometer and, if you
  allow it, the heart rate sensor to find a restless, light-sleep moment to
  wake you. This is the reason for the body sensor / heart rate permissions
  and the background health service.
- **Sleep records from Samsung Health (phone, optional).** If you connect
  Samsung Health, the phone app reads your past sleep sessions and sleep
  stages to show your nights and to check when Lucid woke you. Lucid only
  reads; it never writes to Samsung Health.
- **Alarm settings.** Your wake-up time, window and days.

## Where the data goes

- The data is processed and stored only on your devices, in the app's private
  storage.
- The phone and the watch exchange your alarm settings and a short summary of
  each night (wake time, movement and heart-rate summaries per 30 seconds)
  through Google's Wear OS Data Layer (Google Play services). This connection
  is between your own devices and is encrypted; Lucid does not send the data
  anywhere else.
- Lucid does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use
  it for advertising.

## Keeping and deleting data

Nights are kept on your devices so the app can show your recent sleep. Uninstalling
Lucid from a device deletes everything Lucid stored on that device. Samsung
Health data stays in Samsung Health and is governed by Samsung's policy.

## Permissions

- Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarm and battery settings: to ring
  reliably at the right time.
- Body sensors / heart rate, background health data, high sampling rate
  sensors and a health foreground service (watch): to listen to your sleep
  during the window.
- Samsung Health sleep data (phone, optional): to show your nights.

## Children

Lucid is not directed at children under 13.
